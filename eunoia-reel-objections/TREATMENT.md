# Eunoia reel 2: "But therapy isn't for me…" (treatment)

23 s · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · Living Screencast · second reel in the booking-flow series · the client's own narration (`voiceover-reel2/narration.wav`)

## 1. Three structures

1. **The objection deck (chosen).** One card slot at the top of the frame holds a single inner thought at a time, in quotes. The real screen below answers it while Neha speaks; the thought is struck through in the pause of her line, and the card swipes away. Then the next thought flips in. The last flip turns the card over to the call to action. The deck is the mute-friendly spine: you can follow the whole reel from the cards alone.
2. **Split chat.** The objections arrive as messages from a friend, and the screen answers each one. Rejected: chat bubbles read as another app, and they compete with the booking UI for the same space.
3. **One long scroll.** A single continuous scroll through the site, with the objections as margin notes. Rejected: the notes would be small and secondary, and the brief makes the objection text the hero.

**Why 1:** four beats of about 4 s each need one fixed place for the eye. A single card slot gives a fast, repeatable rhythm (thought → proof → strike → swipe) that reads on mute in under a second per card.

## 2. Logline and arc

*Four reasons not to start therapy, crossed out one by one by the real Eunoia booking screen, ending on a ₹99 first session.*

- **Hook (0–2.2):** "Therapy? Not for me…" is on screen from frame 1. The phone idles below it, and the sprout sits drooped on the card.
- **Four answers (2.2–19.1):** cost → language → privacy → effort. Each objection is struck through as the screen proves it wrong; the sprout droops on each new card and perks up into the phone as it's answered.
- **Call to action (19.1–23):** the card flips one last time, upright, reading "Book your ₹99 first session · eunoia.care". The phone shows the logo, with the sprout sitting on it.

**Tone:** warm. The objections are written as real worries in the viewer's own voice. The strike-through is a gentle marker line, not a red X, and nothing is mocked.

## 5. The sprout

The same sprite as reel 1, at about two-thirds size (4 world px per sprite pixel, 52 × 80 px at base zoom), so it supports and doesn't lead.

- **On each new objection:** it lands on the card's top edge, drooped, eyes down.
- **On the strike:** it perks up (leaves up, happy eyes) and hops down onto the element that answers the worry: the ₹99 block, the Hindi chip, the masked mobile row, "You're booked.".
- **End card:** it sits on the logo, leaves up.

It always stands on a real top edge and never covers text.

**Objection hits:** a flip hit on each card entry (low tom + snap + air whoosh), and a strike hit (a short piano stab plus a marker squeak) on each off-beat strike. Both stay clear of word onsets.

**Mix:** music ducked about 6 dB under the voice, voice about 10 dB over the music, master −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP.
